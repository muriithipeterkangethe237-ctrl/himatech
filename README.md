## 🌐 Overview
**Hemitech Kenya** is a premier technology and networking enterprise specializing in robust infrastructure deployments, enterprise wireless setups, point-to-point bridging, and reliable power backup integrations. We design and implement scalable networks tailored for optimal performance across demanding environments.

---

## 🛠️ Core Capabilities & Technologies

We work with enterprise-grade networking and hardware vendors to deliver high-performance solutions:

*   **Routing & Switching:** MikroTik (RB5009, RB4011, L009, hEX, CCR series) and EdgeRouter architectures.
*   **Wireless & Access Points:** Ruijie/Reyee, Ubiquiti UniFi & airMAX, Grandstream GWN series, and Wavlink outdoor long-range equipment.
*   **Satellite & Connectivity:** Starlink Mini and Gen 3 integration, custom DC power setups, and structural mounting.
*   **Power & Backup Solutions:** Portable power stations, solar backup configurations (Vestwoods, Hithium), and automated solar management.
*   **Captive Portals & Billing:** Customized Wi-Fi landing pages, secure M-Pesa payment integration, and localized access control.
